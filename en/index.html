03R&D

The lab is where the products begin

SyntenIA keeps a permanent research and development front. It was born in genomic surveillance, where a data error becomes a public health error. We bring that standard of traceability to everything else.

Research lines

  1. L1

    Genomic surveillance of antimicrobial resistance

    How to turn thousands of bacterial genomes into trends a health manager can read.

  2. L2

    Pathogen genomics at scale

    Reproducible pipelines to assemble, type and compare genomes without relying on manual work.

  3. L3

    AI with traceable numbers

    LLM-generated reports in which every number points to its source. It is the basis of the AI reports in GeSUS.

  4. L4

    Open government data

    Integration and quality of public health and finance datasets, so official indicators can be recalculated and audited.

  5. L5

    Genomics and sensors in the field

    Low-cost hardware that takes the lab to where the data is born: portable sequencing and connected biosensors.

From research to product

  1. Question

    A real problem from a lab, a company or a health department.

  2. Prototype

    Real data, small scope, a measurable result within weeks.

  3. Open platform

    When it is science, we publish: AMRnet and TyphiNET are open access.

  4. Product

    When it solves a recurring problem, it becomes a SaaS we operate.

04About

A technology company with Amazonian DNA

SyntenIA has Amazonian DNA and works for the world. We do data science, genomics and AI to an international standard, from a region where the problems are concrete and urgent: disease surveillance, public health management, a standing forest.

Three fronts under one roof: consulting for companies and government, our own products in operation, and an R&D lab that feeds both.

The team is multidisciplinary: data scientists, software engineers, DevOps, data architects, physicians, a sanitary engineer and an environmental engineer, working together from data to decision.

Leadership

Dr Louise Cerdeira

CEO and founder

Bioinformatician and data scientist. She created the AMRnet and TyphiNET platforms, used in genomic surveillance of antimicrobial resistance and typhoid fever.

Her career combines research at institutions such as USP, Monash University and the Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine with technology projects at companies such as Fleury, Salomão Zoppi, Vale and Tesla.

On every project, the team takes part in the initial cartography and is accountable for the architecture.

Dr Sarah Scalercio

Director of Innovation

Pharmacist with a master's degree and a PhD. Postdoctoral researcher at the Faculty of Biomedicine of the Federal University of Pará, with a track record at the Evandro Chagas Institute and the National Primate Centre.

Her research in reproductive biology uses Amazonian primates as a model: ovarian tissue cryopreservation, the first step towards an artificial ovary on a 3D fibrin scaffold, and the damage Zika infection causes in pregnancy and in the testes.
